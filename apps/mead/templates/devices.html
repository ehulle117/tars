Setting one up

  1. Flash GravityMon (or use the stock iSpindel firmware) and join your WiFi.
  2. Calibrate it against your hydrometer in water and a sugar solution, as its docs describe, with gravity in SG.
  3. Under push targets, add HTTP POST to {{ request.base_url }}api/ispindel with the iSpindel format. On your home WiFi, http://192.168.8.103:8770/api/ispindel works too.
  4. Set the interval to 900 seconds (15 minutes) for a good battery life.
  5. Once it shows up here, type its name into the batch's Details → iSpindel.
